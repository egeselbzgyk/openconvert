The office was quiet at that hour, and the clerk who remained at his desk did not look up when the door opened, nor when it closed again behind the visitor.

He was copying a schedule of freight rates in a hand so regular that the page might have been printed, and he had been copying it since the middle of the afternoon.1

The Visitor

The visitor waited. There is a kind of patience that is really a kind of pressure, and he had it.

When at last the clerk spoke, it was to say that the schedule would be finished by six, and that nothing whatever could be done before then.2
