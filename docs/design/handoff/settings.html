AI assistance

What AI assistance does, and what it costs

Conversion is complete without it. When it's on, a small model on this computer helps with four once-per-book decisions: ambiguous heading levels, book structure (where front matter and chapters begin), and telling verse and quotations apart from prose.

Costs:~1,100 MB download~1.5–2 GB RAM while convertinga few extra seconds per book

Each result shows how many decisions it made ("AI-assisted decisions: 4"), and the report lists each one next to what the rules alone would have chosen.

AI assistance
Off. Turning it on opens the model download if no model is installed.
