40-file rule: exactly one job runs. Each waiting row shows its position and a Remove button. Removing a waiting job needs no confirmation; it disappears and positions renumber (announced: "parish-register-1866.pdf removed. 38 waiting."). "Remove all waiting…" asks once. Timing contract: Cancel → "Cancelling…" at once → "Cancelled" when done{status:"cancelled"} arrives, normally within 2 s. At 5 s the supervisor kills the job and the row turns "Cancelled" anyway. No heartbeat for more than 6 s → "Not responding". When heartbeats return, the row goes back to its stage label.
