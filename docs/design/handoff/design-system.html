Design system: foundations and components

Every component below uses only tokens.css and oc.css. Each light pane is mirrored as dark by setting data-theme="dark". In the app, dark mode comes from prefers-color-scheme alone. States that normally need a pointer are forced with .is-hover, .is-active and .is-focus.

Separation and elevation
Separation comes from the 1 px border and one step of surface colour. The 1 px shadow is decoration only (none in dark), so nothing depends on it. There is no blur or backdrop-filter anywhere.
Icons: Lucide (ISC), stroke 1.75, 1 / 1.125 / 1.25 / 1.75 rem
Focus: 2 px solid focus, 2 px offset, follows the radius. :focus-visible only.
